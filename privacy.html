Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

BrightHarbor Press is a small independent publisher. This page explains what we collect when you visit brightharborpress.com, why we collect it, and how to have it removed. We have tried to write it in plain English rather than legal boilerplate.

The short version

We collect your name and email address only if you choose to join our newsletter. We do not sell or rent your information, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not set any cookies of our own. You can ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing hello@brightharborpress.com.

Who we are

BrightHarbor Press, United States. For anything in this policy, contact us at hello@brightharborpress.com.

What we collect

Newsletter details. If you fill in the sign-up form, we receive the name and email address you enter. That is the only information you actively give us. There is no account to create and no other form on the site.

Basic server records. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical details of requests, such as IP address and browser type, in order to serve the site and protect it from abuse. We do not control these records and do not use them to identify individual readers. GitHub's handling of that data is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts. The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those files directly from Google, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request. No cookie is set for this.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We do not set advertising or analytics cookies.
  • We do not build profiles of readers or track you across other websites.
  • We do not ask for payment details. All purchases happen on Amazon, not here.

Links to Amazon

Every "Buy on Amazon" button takes you to Amazon's website. Once you leave our site, Amazon's own privacy policy and cookies apply, not ours. We do not receive your Amazon account details, your order information, or anything else about what you do there.

How we use your details

We use your name and email address for one purpose: to send you occasional news about new releases from BrightHarbor Press. We do not use it for anything else, and we do not pass it to other publishers or marketers.

If you are in the UK or EU, our lawful basis for this is your consent, which you give by submitting the form and can withdraw at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your newsletter details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. Once you do, we remove your details from our list.

Who else can see it

To send email we rely on an email delivery service, which stores the list on our behalf and may not share or sell it. We will name that provider here once it is in place. Other than that provider, and anyone we are legally required to disclose to, nobody else receives your details.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also simply reply and ask.
  • See what we hold. Email us and we will tell you what is on our list for you.
  • Correct or delete it. Email us and we will fix or remove it.

If you are in the UK or EU, you also have rights to object to processing, to restrict it, and to receive your data in a portable form, and you may complain to your national data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we hold, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell it. We will never treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

Younger readers

Our books are written for readers aged roughly fourteen and up, and this site is not directed at children under thirteen. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under thirteen. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us their details, email us and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

If we start using analytics, advertising tools, or a different email provider, we will update this page and change the date at the top before those tools go live. Material changes will be reflected here rather than announced separately.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything we hold: hello@brightharborpress.com.
